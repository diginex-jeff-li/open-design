The business challenge

Blind spots in supply chains are costing more than compliance. You can't fix what you can't see.

  • Regulations (AU, UK, Canada) now require supply chain transparency.
  • Exploitative labor conditions and environmental damage often go hand-in-hand.
  • In-person audits often miss hidden issues—too costly, too shallow.
  • Workers rarely feel safe speaking up in person.
  • Poor visibility = compliance risk, reputational damage, and disruption.
