Design tokens, quietly systematised.

Diginex is a software-native UI on Nuxt UI v4 and Tailwind v4 — white canvas, slate neutrals, a single lilac accent, and hairline depth.

Active

Color

Seven ramps, 50–950. Primary and secondary derived in OKLCH from the real 500 anchors.

Typography

DM Sans display, Open Sans body, JetBrains Mono for code. Medium 500 is the UI default.

Depth

Hairline 1px borders and ring shadows — no heavy drop shadows on flat surfaces.
